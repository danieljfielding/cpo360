The challenge

Procurement has a visibility problem.

Most CPOs are doing great work. But without the right tools, it is hard to demonstrate it — to your team, your stakeholders, or your Board.

01

No single source of truth

Spend data is messy. Categories are inconsistent. There is no clean view of what you are buying, from whom, and at what cost.

02

Hard to show the Board what good looks like

CEOs and CFOs want evidence that procurement is a strategic function. Without a reporting layer, that story is hard to tell.

03

Team capability gaps stay hidden

Without structured assessment, it is hard to know where your team excels, where they need development, and how that compares to best practice.

One view. Everything that matters.

The toolkit

Nine tools. One integrated platform.

Each module works standalone. Together, they give you a complete picture of your procurement function.

01

Category strategy & spend intelligence

Build category strategies backed by real data. Identify savings opportunities, track benefits delivery, and generate AI-powered strategy narratives.

Spend analysis · Kraljic matrix · AI narratives

02

Procurement skills assessment

Assess your team across 10 procurement competencies. Surface capability gaps and build a development roadmap grounded in evidence, not guesswork.

90 questions · 10 competencies · Benchmarking

03

Maturity assessment & transformation roadmap

Understand where your function stands across 12 dimensions. Compare to industry benchmarks and build a prioritised improvement roadmap.

12 dimensions · Benchmarks · Roadmap

04

Clean, categorised spend data

Upload raw spend data, cleanse it with AI-powered supplier normalisation and taxonomy classification, then interrogate it to support strategy.

AI classification · Rules library · Drill-down

05

Stakeholder satisfaction

Understand how your internal customers feel about working with procurement. Identify what is working and where the relationship needs attention.

NPS tracking · Feedback themes

06

Supplier relationship health

Find out how your key suppliers experience working with your organisation. Spot relationship risks before they become commercial problems.

Supplier NPS · Segment views

07

Carbon footprint & modern slavery risk

Understand your Scope 3 emissions profile and modern slavery exposure across your supply base. Report with confidence to your Board and regulators.

Scope 3 · Supplier mapping

08

Value tracker

Track savings initiatives, benefits delivery and procurement's contribution to the P&L. Give your CFO and Board a credible performance picture.

Savings pipeline · Board reporting

09

eLearning

On-demand eLearning across 12 procurement topics. Learn at your own pace, track progress, and build team capability systematically over time.

12 topics · Team dashboards
